The official website for Hobo's Pi Hole

What is Plex?

Plex is an ad-supported streaming service that also supports self-hosting your own personal media. I have a Media Server named Hobo's Pi Hole running off of a Raspberry Pi 4 in my router closet, hooked up to a 4tb external HDD. What this means for you is 24/7 uptime and a virtually unlimited number of movies and television at your disposal.

How do I join?

  • Go to plex.tv
  • Sign up
  • DM me the email you used to sign up, and I will invite you!
